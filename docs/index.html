Everything you need, nothing you do not

The first version covers the daily loop of a personal ledger, from the moment a payment happens to the moment it sits in your sheet.

Automatic capture

Payment alerts from the common Indian payment apps and bank debit alerts are picked up as they arrive. Each app has its own switch.

One tap categories

A small sheet pops up over whatever you are doing. Pick the category and you are done. The app learns the ones you use most.

Equal and custom splits

Split a bill equally with friends or type your own share. The app tracks who owes you until they settle, and what you owe others.

Your own Google Sheet

One workbook per year in your Drive, with a layout you can read, filter and chart. The app can only open files it created.

Needs Review

When an alert is unclear, the entry waits in Needs Review instead of guessing. Unconfirmed entries are never counted as spending.

Budgets, trips and subscriptions

Track a monthly budget, tag a trip, remember recurring bills, and see where the money went on the dashboard and in Insights.

How it works

Three steps, and only the first one is yours.

You pay

Use any payment app as you normally do. A notification arrives on your phone.

You tap once

The app reads the alert on the device, fills in the amount and payee, and asks only for the category.

It lands in your sheet

The entry is saved on the phone first, then synced to your Google Sheet as soon as it can.

An offline queue, so nothing is lost

Every change is written to the database on your phone first and placed in a sync queue. If you are on a train with no signal, you lose nothing.

  • Changes sync in order when the network is back.
  • Brief problems such as no network or a busy server are retried automatically, up to a limit.
  • If an item still fails, it appears in Needs Review with the reason, and you choose Retry or Discard.
  • Each row carries a hidden id, so a retry never creates a duplicate in your sheet.

Privacy was the first requirement, not the last

Your financial data is yours. The design makes it hard for anyone else to see it, including the developer.

Parsed on your phone onlyAlerts are read in memory on the device. The raw message text is never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

Stored on the phone and in your DriveEntries live in an encrypted database on the phone and in your own Google Sheet. No other user and not the developer can see them.

No server in the middleThere is no backend that receives your transactions. Your settings, such as accounts, categories and friends, are saved on the phone and in a Config tab of your own sheet.

The narrowest Google accessThe app asks only for the drive.file permission, which limits it to files it created itself.

Chat apps stay blockedPersonal messaging apps are never read. WhatsApp is read only when its own notification channel marks a message as a payment.

Reports are opt in and anonymousCrash reports and usage counts are off until you turn them on. A problem report is sent only when you tap Send after seeing exactly what it contains.

Built in the open

The app is written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, an encrypted local database and an offline first sync engine, and it is covered by about 1,500 unit tests. The code, the sheet layout and the privacy rules are all public under the MIT License.
